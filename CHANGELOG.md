## 0.3.0

- **First version.** Every Mythic+ dungeon and raid teleport in one window, listed by
  the place it takes you to rather than by the portal's name — "Ara-Kara, City of
  Echoes" first, "Path of the Ruined City" underneath.
- **Three sections: This season, Dungeons, Raids**, each in its own colour. This season
  lists the current Mythic+ dungeons with their portals, wherever in the game those
  live; Dungeons and Raids are grouped by expansion, newest first. The header says which
  expansion and season it is.
- **This season's portals beside the Mythic+ page.** Open the Mythic+ Dungeons tab of the
  Group Finder and a small panel docks beside it listing this season's dungeons — click
  one to teleport there. It closes with the page, has its own close button for when you
  just want it gone this time, and can be switched off from the main window or with
  `/np attach`. It only reads Blizzard's window to line up with it and never changes it.
- **Everything folds.** Click a section or an expansion to fold it; the earned count
  stays visible when folded, and the window remembers what you folded. A search looks
  inside folded groups too.
- **Click a portal to teleport.** Ones you have not earned are listed greyed out, so you
  can see what exists; a button hides them.
- **Search** by dungeon, portal or expansion.
- Shows each portal's cooldown, counting down while the window is open.
- One row per destination: where the game lists an Alliance and a Horde spell for the
  same door, you see one row, the one you can cast.
- Read from the game rather than from a list: a new season or expansion appears with no
  update needed.
- `/np` (or `/nport`) to open it, a minimap button, and a keybinding.
